About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University. My research centers on medical image analysis across PET, CT, and MRI, with a focus on segmentation, image generation, and neural representation. Before my Ph.D., I worked on deep learning for nuclear medicine and quantitative imaging at Asan Medical Center and Samsung Medical Center.
