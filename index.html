About

My research interests began in medical image processing and have gradually expanded toward artificial intelligence. During my master's program I worked on PET/CT imaging and segmentation through projects at major academic medical centers in Korea. In my Ph.D. I have been working on MRI and medical image generation.

This path has made me interested not only in medical imaging but also in how AI models learn from data and can be used to solve different problems. Recently I have been working on efficient and controllable image generation, focusing on important information while reducing unnecessary computation. I am interested in developing new methods and learning strategies while keeping real-world usefulness in mind.
